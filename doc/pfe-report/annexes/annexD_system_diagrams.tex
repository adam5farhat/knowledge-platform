\chapter{Complete system diagrams}
\label{annex:diagrams}

This appendix provides the \textbf{structural design views} and a \textbf{sequence-diagram
catalog} for the Knowledge Platform. Structural figures are reproduced here for oral
defense convenience; canonical in-text citations are in
Chapters~\ref{ch:requirements} (global use-case model) and~\ref{ch:architecture}
(architecture and class diagram). The \textbf{41 interaction sequence
diagrams} are presented in full by sprint in
Section~\ref{sec:sprint-design} (Chapter~\ref{ch:architecture}); this appendix
indexes them for oral defense and traceability.
